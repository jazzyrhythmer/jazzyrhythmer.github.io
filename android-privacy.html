Android app privacy

This policy covers JR EMUL, JR REVERB, JR DELAY, JR ECHO, JR FX and JR ACID for Android, developed by Jazzy Rhythmer.

Audio and local data

The apps process audio on your device. They use local storage for app settings and, where supported, imported sounds, presets and recordings. The apps do not require a user account and do not send your audio, MIDI, settings or recordings to the developer.

JR EMUL and JR FX use microphone permission for live input. JR FX can display a foreground-service notification when background audio is active. JR ACID can receive MIDI from devices or apps you connect. JR REVERB, JR DELAY and JR ECHO use built-in demo loops and do not request microphone access.

Sharing and retention

EMUL and FX can open or save audio through your chosen Android file provider. That provider handles its own storage and data processing. You can remove app-local data in Android Settings, and delete exported copies at the location you chose. Clearing app storage or uninstalling an app can remove local settings and recordings.

If you email support, Jazzy Rhythmer receives the message and any files you attach and uses them to respond. You can contact us to request deletion of support correspondence. Google Play handles app distribution and any Play purchases under its own privacy policy.
